Quick Start Guide

Get up and running in about 5 minutes

What you'll accomplish: By the end of this guide, BirthdayMessaging will be installed, your account will be set up, and the extension will be ready to automatically send personalized birthday messages to your Facebook friends.
1 Install the Extension
  1. Open Chrome and go to the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Search for BirthdayMessaging and click Add to Chrome.
  3. Confirm by clicking Add extension in the dialog.
  4. The 🎂 birthday cake icon will appear in your Chrome toolbar. If you don't see it, click the puzzle-piece icon and pin BirthdayMessaging.
2 Create Your Account
  1. Click the 🎂 icon in your toolbar to open the extension.
  2. Click Create an account.
  3. Enter your email address and a password (minimum 8 characters).
  4. Click Register. You'll be logged in automatically.
Already have an account? Click Sign in instead and enter your credentials.
3 Create a Message Template

Templates are the birthday messages sent automatically. You can create multiple — BirthdayMessaging picks one at random each day for variety.

  1. Go to the Messages tab → Templates.
  2. Click + New Template.
  3. Fill in the three fields:
FieldExampleMax length
GreetingHappy Birthday, [Tag Person]! 🎉300 chars
BodyWishing you an amazing day filled with joy!1000 chars
Closing— Your friend300 chars
  1. (Optional) Set a Gender filter if this template is better suited to a specific audience.
  2. Click Save Template.

Personalization tags you can use

TagReplaced with
[Tag Person]The friend's name (Facebook @mention)
[Today]Today's date (e.g. March 18, 2026)
4 Set a Schedule

Schedules tell the extension when to send messages relative to a friend's birthday.

  1. Go to Messages → Scheduling.
  2. Click + New Schedule.
  3. Set Days Before:
    • 0 — send on the birthday itself
    • 1 (positive) — send a day early
    • -1 (negative) — send a follow-up the day after
  4. Toggle the schedule On.
  5. Click Save.
5 Let Automation Run
  1. Go to Messages → Automation.
  2. Confirm the status shows Active.
  3. Make sure you are logged in to Facebook in your browser.
  4. The extension will automatically check Facebook's birthday feed at your scheduled times and send messages on your behalf.
Manual trigger: Click Run Check Now to immediately scan for today's birthdays without waiting for the next scheduled run.

You're Done! 🎉

BirthdayMessaging will now automatically:

  • Detect friends with birthdays on Facebook
  • Compose a personalized message using your templates
  • Send messages via Facebook Messenger
  • Log all activity in Messages → History

What's Next?

  • Add a timeline post (Messages → Timeline Posts) to also post on friends' walls.
  • Import contacts (Contacts tab) to manage friends and their details manually.
  • Review sent messages in Messages → History to see what was sent and when.
  • Upgrade your subscription in Account → Subscription to unlock more messages per day.
