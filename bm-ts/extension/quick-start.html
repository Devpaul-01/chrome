BirthdayMessaging

Quick Start Guide

Get up and running in about 5 minutes

What you'll accomplish: By the end of this guide, BirthdayMessaging will be installed, your account will be set up, your contacts imported from Facebook, and the extension ready to automatically send personalised birthday messages to your Facebook friends.
⚠️ Facebook must be open. BirthdayMessaging works by reading the Facebook page that is open in your browser — it cannot send messages or scan birthdays unless Chrome is running and you are logged in to Facebook. Keep Chrome open and make sure your Facebook session hasn't expired.
1 Install the Extension
  1. Open Chrome and go to the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Search for BirthdayMessaging and click Add to Chrome.
  3. Confirm by clicking Add extension in the dialog.
  4. The 🎂 birthday cake icon will appear in your Chrome toolbar. If you don't see it, click the puzzle-piece icon and pin BirthdayMessaging.
2 Create Your Account
  1. Click the 🎂 icon in your toolbar to open the extension popup.
  2. Click Create an account.
  3. Enter your email address and a password (minimum 8 characters).
  4. Click Register. You'll be logged in automatically.
Already have an account? Click Sign in instead and enter your credentials.
3 Open Facebook Birthdays & Populate Contacts

Before anything else, BirthdayMessaging needs to scan Facebook to find your friends' birthdays and build your contacts list. This is done directly from the Facebook birthdays page.

  1. In Chrome, open a new tab and navigate to facebook.com/birthdays .
  2. In the extension popup, go to the Home tab and click the Facebook Popup button. This opens the BirthdayMessaging overlay directly on the Facebook birthdays page.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Facebook birthdays page so that all upcoming birthdays are loaded.
  4. In the overlay, click Refresh. The extension will scan the page, detect all visible birthdays, and automatically import them as contacts.
Tip: Run this scan regularly (e.g. weekly) to keep your contacts list up to date as new birthdays come into view on Facebook.
4 Create Groups

Groups let you organise your contacts and assign different message templates to different sets of people. For example, you might have a Close Friends group with warm, personal messages and a Colleagues group with more professional ones.

When automation runs, the extension selects a template from the group assigned to each contact, keeping messages relevant and personalised without any manual work.

  1. Go to the Create tab → Groups.
  2. Click New Group and give it a descriptive name (e.g. "Close Friends").
  3. Save the group. Repeat for as many categories as you need.
  4. Assign contacts to groups from the Contacts view by editing a contact and selecting a group.
Recommended: Create at least a Default group for general contacts and one or two specialist groups (e.g. family, colleagues) before creating templates.
5 Upload Images

Images are stored in your Image Library and can be inserted into any message template or timeline post using the [Image] keyword. When a message is sent, a random image from your library is used — adding variety to your messages.

  1. Go to the Create tab → Image Library.
  2. Click Upload Image and select one or more images from your device.
  3. Upload at least a few images so the [Image] keyword has variety to draw from.
6 Create Message Templates

Message templates are sent as private Facebook direct messages on a friend's birthday. You can create as many as you like per group — the extension picks one at random each time, which keeps your messages varied and helps avoid Facebook flagging your account for sending repetitive content.

Important — create at least 3 templates per group. Facebook may temporarily restrict accounts that repeatedly send identical messages. Having 3 or more templates per group ensures the extension always has enough variety to rotate through, significantly reducing the risk of restrictions.
  1. Go to the Create tab → Message Templates.
  2. Fill in the Greeting, Body, and Closing fields.
  3. (Optional) Add [Image] anywhere in the body to insert a random image from your library.
  4. Select the Group this template belongs to (e.g. "Close Friends").
  5. Click Save Template.
  6. Repeat until you have at least 3 templates for each group.

Personalization tags you can use

Tag Replaced with
[Tag Person] The friend's name (Facebook @mention) — for message templates
{First_Name} The friend's first name — for timeline posts
{Last_Name} The friend's last name — for timeline posts
[Today] / {Today} Today's date (e.g. March 18, 2026)
[Image] A random image from your Image Library
7 (Optional) Create Timeline Posts

Timeline posts are written directly on a friend's Facebook wall on their birthday, in addition to (or instead of) a direct message. Create them from the Create tab → Timeline Posts.

Use the same personalization tags as message templates. The {First_Name} and {Last_Name} tags are particularly useful for timeline posts.

8 Set a Schedule

Schedules tell the extension when to send messages relative to a friend's birthday.

  1. Go to the Scheduling tab.
  2. Under Birthday Message Schedule, choose a timing mode:
  3. Set the timing:
    • On the birthday — send on the actual day
    • Days before — send a message early (e.g. 1 day before)
    • Days after — send a follow-up (e.g. "Hope you had a great birthday!")
  4. Click Add Schedule.
9 Enable Automation & Go

Once your contacts, groups, and templates are set up, automation handles everything else. When the automation engine is active, the extension periodically checks Facebook for birthdays and sends messages using your templates — without you needing to do anything.

  1. In the Scheduling tab, scroll to Automation Settings.
  2. Choose a Check Interval (how often to scan for birthdays — default is 1 hour).
  3. Set your preferred Send Confirmation mode:
    • Each message — asks every time (safest)
    • Per session — asks once per run
    • Auto — sends without asking (higher ban risk — use only with plenty of templates per group)
  4. Go to the Home tab and turn the Automation Engine toggle to ON. Chrome will show a permission prompt — click Allow to grant the alarm scheduling permission required for background birthday checks.
  5. Make sure Chrome is open, you are logged in to Facebook, and the facebook.com/birthdays page is accessible in your browser. The extension needs this page to detect birthdays.
Manual trigger: Click Run Check Now in Automation Settings to immediately scan for today's birthdays without waiting for the next scheduled run. You can also open the Facebook Popup on the birthdays page at any time for manual sending.

You're All Set! 🎉

BirthdayMessaging will now automatically:

  • Detect friends with birthdays on Facebook
  • Pick a random template from the correct group for each contact
  • Send a personalised message via Facebook Messenger
  • Optionally post on their timeline
  • Log all activity in Message History

The Facebook Overlay

When you open the Facebook Popup on facebook.com/birthdays, BirthdayMessaging adds a floating sidebar overlay directly on the page. This gives you manual control alongside the automated system.

What's in the overlay

Section Description
Status dot Green = automation active. Yellow = rate-limited or outside preferred hours. Red = emergency stop. Grey = disabled.
Friend list Checkboxes for each friend whose birthday is today. Select who to message.
Feature toggles DM = send a direct message, Post = post on their timeline, Archive = archive the chat after sending.
Template preview Shows the message that will be sent. Click 🔀 to shuffle to a different template.
Send to all Sends messages to all checked friends with a random delay between each.
Stats pills Messages sent vs. your safety caps. Sending pauses automatically when a cap is reached.

Overlay controls

  • Refresh — re-scan the page for birthday friends and reload templates. Also use this after scrolling to the bottom of the birthdays page to import all contacts.
  • ▶ (per-friend) — send a message to just that one person.
  • Stop — appears while sending; halts after the current message finishes.
  • ? (help) — toggles built-in tips inside the overlay.
  • − / + — minimise or expand the panel.
Tip: Drag the overlay's title bar to reposition it anywhere on the page. Press Escape to hide it entirely.

What's Next?

  • Review sent messages in the Message Queue and Message History views.
  • Check the Dashboard (Home → Analytics) for delivery stats and trends.
  • Upgrade your subscription in Account → Subscription to unlock more messages per day.

Month-Only Birthdays

Facebook only shows the exact birthday date for friends whose birthday is coming up soon. For other friends, BirthdayMessaging can only detect the birth month — not the exact day. These contacts are marked with a "Month only" label in your Contacts list.

Important: Automated messaging is paused for month-only contacts to prevent messages being sent on the wrong day. To resolve this:
  • Wait until the friend's birthday is approaching and re-scan the Facebook birthdays page — BirthdayMessaging will automatically update the contact with the exact date.
  • Or manually edit the contact in the Contacts tab and enter the correct birthday.
