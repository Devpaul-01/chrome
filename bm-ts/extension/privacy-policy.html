Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 18, 2026  ·  Last updated: March 18, 2026

Summary: BirthdayMessaging collects only what is needed to automate birthday messages on your behalf. We never sell your data, and you can export or delete everything at any time.

1. Who We Are

BirthdayMessaging ("we", "us", "our") is a Chrome browser extension and associated backend service operated by CTM Media Group. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and what rights you have.

Questions or requests: support@birthdaymessaging.io

2. What Data We Collect

2a. Account Data

  • Email address — used to identify your account and send service emails (e.g. password reset).
  • Password — stored as a one-way Argon2 hash; we cannot recover or read your plaintext password.
  • Display name and timezone — optional profile fields you may set in Account Settings.

2b. Facebook Contact Data

  • Friend names and profile URLs — collected by the extension from Facebook's birthday feed when you run automation.
  • Birthday dates (month and day) — read from Facebook to schedule messages.
  • Gender selection — set by you in Contact Management to choose gender-aware message templates.

2c. Message Content

  • Message templates — the greeting, body, and closing text you write and save.
  • Message queue — scheduled outbound messages before they are sent.
  • Message history — a log of every message attempt, including outcome and any error.

2d. Configuration Data

  • Birthday schedules — how many days before or after a birthday to send a message.
  • SMTP settings — optional email server credentials you provide for notification emails. Passwords are encrypted at rest using AES-256 (Fernet).
  • Automation preferences — message limits, delays, emergency stop state — stored locally in chrome.storage.local.

2e. Subscription and Payment Data

  • Stripe customer ID and subscription status — we store a reference to your Stripe record. We do not store card numbers; Stripe handles all payment data directly.

2f. Technical Data

  • Server logs — standard HTTP access logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) retained for up to 30 days for security and debugging.
  • Audit log entries — records of sensitive actions (login, password change, account deletion) associated with your user ID, with no message content.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not read your private Facebook messages.
  • We do not store your Facebook login credentials.
  • We do not track your browsing history outside of facebook.com during automation.
  • We do not use cookies for analytics or advertising.
  • We do not collect data from any website other than facebook.com.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Account data — retained until you delete your account.
  • Message history — retained until you delete individual records or delete your account.
  • Server logs — automatically deleted after 30 days.
  • Revoked tokens — expired entries are purged after their token expiry date.
  • After account deletion, all your data is permanently removed from our database. Backups are purged within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection law (including GDPR) you have the right to:

  • Access your data — use GET /auth/me (from the extension) or send a written request to support@birthdaymessaging.io.
  • Export your data — download a complete copy of all your data at any time via Account → Export My Data in the extension, or by calling GET /auth/data-export. The export includes your profile, templates, schedules, contacts, and full message history in JSON format.
  • Rectification — update your profile, contacts, and templates directly in the extension.
  • Erasure (right to be forgotten) — permanently delete your account and all associated data via Account → Delete Account in the extension, or by calling DELETE /auth/account. Deletion is irreversible.
  • Restrict processing — contact us at support@birthdaymessaging.io to suspend processing of your data while a dispute is being resolved.
  • Object to processing — contact us to object to any processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Lodge a complaint — if you are in the EEA, you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

We will respond to all data subject requests within 30 days.

7. Chrome Extension Permissions

BirthdayMessaging requests the following Chrome permissions. Each permission is strictly necessary for the operation of the extension:

Permission Why it is needed
storage Save your authentication tokens and automation preferences locally in Chrome so you stay logged in between sessions.
scripting Inject the automation content script into Facebook pages so the extension can detect birthday friends and trigger message sending.
activeTab Access the currently active tab to execute automation when you trigger it manually from the popup.
tabs Query open Chrome tabs to locate and reuse an existing Facebook tab during automated birthday checks, avoiding unnecessary new tab creation.
alarms Schedule a periodic background check (once per day) to check for upcoming birthdays and queue messages without requiring the popup to be open.
clipboardWrite Fill message input fields and attach images via the clipboard API during automation. This permission is only used while the extension is actively sending birthday messages.
host_permissions: *://*.facebook.com/* The core purpose of the extension: read the Facebook birthday feed and interact with Messenger to send personalised birthday messages on your behalf.

8. Security

We apply the following technical and organisational measures to protect your data:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+.
  • Passwords are hashed using Argon2id (memory-hard, OWASP-recommended).
  • SMTP passwords are encrypted at rest using AES-256 (Fernet symmetric encryption).
  • JWTs use short-lived access tokens (15 minutes) with refresh token rotation and revocation.
  • Rate limiting is applied to all authentication endpoints to prevent brute-force attacks.
  • Accounts are automatically locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.
  • Security headers (HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options, etc.) are applied on all API responses.
  • Input sanitisation is applied to all user-submitted text fields to prevent injection attacks.
  • Sensitive operations (login, password change, account deletion) are recorded in an audit log.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

The extension does not set cookies in your browser. Authentication tokens and local preferences are stored in chrome.storage.local, which is private to the extension and inaccessible to websites. No data is stored in localStorage or sessionStorage.

The backend API does not set cookies. Authentication is handled exclusively via JWT bearer tokens in the Authorization header.

10. Children's Privacy

BirthdayMessaging is not intended for use by persons under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at support@birthdaymessaging.io.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data is stored on servers within the cloud provider's data centres. If those servers are located outside the European Economic Area, transfers are protected by the cloud provider's Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards recognised by applicable data protection law.
