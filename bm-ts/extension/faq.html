Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about BirthdayMessaging

General

1. What does BirthdayMessaging do?

BirthdayMessaging is a Chrome extension that automatically sends personalized birthday messages to your Facebook friends. It detects birthdays from Facebook's birthday feed, selects a message template you've created, and sends a Messenger message — and optionally a timeline post — on your behalf.

2. What data does the extension collect?

The extension collects only what's needed to provide the service:

  • Your email address and hashed password (for your account)
  • Message templates you create
  • Your schedule settings
  • A list of Facebook friends synced from your birthday feed (name + profile URL)
  • A log of messages sent

We do not collect your Facebook password, payment card numbers (handled by Stripe), or any personal data beyond what is listed above. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

3. Is my Facebook account safe?

BirthdayMessaging uses your existing logged-in Facebook session — it does not store your Facebook credentials. The extension mimics natural human interaction (random delays, one message at a time) and applies built-in rate limits (10 per hour, 20 per day, 50 per week) to minimise the risk of triggering Facebook's automation detection.

Your Send Confirmation mode also matters:

  • Each message — shows a preview before every send (safest, recommended)
  • Per session — asks once at the start of each automation run
  • Auto — sends without any confirmation and carries the highest ban risk. The extension displays a prominent warning when this mode is selected. Use only if you have plenty of varied templates and fully understand the risk.

Use at your own discretion, and keep Max Messages Per Run at a reasonable level.

4. How does automation work?

Once you set up a schedule, the extension runs a background check at your configured timing. It opens (or focuses) Facebook's birthday page, scans for today's birthdays, looks up your templates, and sends a message for each friend who hasn't already received one today. A deduplication guard prevents double-sending. All activity is logged in Message History (Message Queue tab).

Account & Billing

5. Do I need a subscription?

A subscription is required to send more than a small number of messages per day. You can subscribe in Account → Subscription. Payments are securely processed by Stripe — we never see or store your card details.

6. How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Account → Subscription and follow the cancellation steps. Your access remains active until the end of the current billing period.

7. How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, click Forgot password?. Enter your registered email address and you'll receive a reset link by email. The link expires after 1 hour. Check your spam folder if the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

8. Can I change my email address?

Yes. Go to Account → Profile and update your email address. Note that changing your email may require re-verification.

Templates & Messages

9. What are merge tags?

Merge tags are placeholders in your message templates that are automatically replaced with real values when a message is sent:

Tag Used in Replaced with
[Tag Person] Message templates The friend's name (@mention on Facebook)
{First_Name} Timeline posts The friend's first name
{Last_Name} Timeline posts The friend's last name
[Today] / {Today} Both Today's date (e.g. March 18, 2026)
[Image] Both A randomly selected image from your Image Library

10. Can I have multiple templates?

Yes! You can create as many templates as you like. BirthdayMessaging randomly selects one each time a message is sent, so your friends see variety. Create templates with different tones (formal, casual, humorous) to keep things fresh.

11. How do I pause automation without deleting my schedule?

Go to the Scheduling tab → Automation Settings and click Pause All Activity 🛑. This pauses all automation for 24 hours. To resume earlier, click the clear button next to the stop notice. You can also toggle the Automation Engine off from the Home tab.

12. Why didn't a message send on a friend's birthday?

Common reasons:

  • Automation was paused — check the Home tab or the Scheduling → Automation Settings status
  • The friend's birthday wasn't visible on Facebook's birthday feed that day
  • The daily message limit was reached — check Max Messages Per Run in Automation settings
  • Chrome wasn't running at the scheduled time — the extension requires Chrome to be open
  • You weren't logged in to Facebook when the check ran

Check Message History and Message Queue for more detail.

Contacts & Facebook

13. How do contacts get added?

Contacts are automatically synced from Facebook's birthday feed when automation runs. You can also add contacts manually in the Contacts tab, or import a list from a CSV, JSON, or VCF file using the Import button.

14. Why do some contacts show "Month only"?

Facebook only displays the exact birthday date for friends whose birthday is coming up in the next few days. For everyone else, only the birth month is visible. When you import contacts, BirthdayMessaging marks these as month-only and automatically pauses messaging for them — this prevents messages from being sent on the wrong day.

15. How do I fix a month-only contact?

There are two ways:

  • Re-import from Facebook when the friend's birthday is approaching. Open facebook.com/friends/birthdays, go to Contacts → Import → Facebook, and re-import. BirthdayMessaging will automatically detect the exact date and update the contact — no need to delete and re-add.
  • Edit the contact manually in the Contacts tab — click Edit, enter the correct birthday, and save. Messaging will resume immediately.

16. What if Facebook changes its layout and automation breaks?

Facebook occasionally updates its UI. If automation stops detecting birthdays or sending messages correctly, check for an extension update in Chrome. While waiting for an update, you can still trigger messages manually using Run Check Now in Automation settings.

We monitor Facebook layout changes and release fixes as quickly as possible.

Facebook Overlay

17. What is the Facebook overlay?

When you visit any Facebook page, BirthdayMessaging injects a floating sidebar directly on the page. It lists today's birthday friends with checkboxes, shows a template preview, and lets you manually send messages without leaving Facebook.

18. How do I use the overlay to send messages manually?

  1. Navigate to facebook.com/friends/birthdays.
  2. The overlay appears on the right side of the page. Click Refresh to detect today's birthday friends.
  3. Use the checkboxes to select which friends should receive a message.
  4. Review the template preview — click 🔀 to shuffle to a different template.
  5. Use the feature toggles to choose what to send: DM (direct message), Post (timeline post), or Archive (archive the chat after sending).
  6. Click Send to all to send messages to all selected friends, or click ▶ on a single friend's row to send only to them.
Safety tip: Messages are sent one at a time with a random delay between each to mimic natural behaviour. Keep sending to 10 or fewer friends per hour.

19. What do the status dot colours mean?

Colour Meaning
Green Automation is active and running normally.
Yellow Rate-limited or outside your preferred sending hours.
Red Emergency stop is active — all sending is paused.
Grey Automation is disabled.

20. How do I hide, move, or minimise the overlay?

  • Hide: Press Escape while the overlay is focused.
  • Move: Click and drag the title bar to reposition it anywhere on the page.
  • Minimise: Click the − button in the top-right corner to collapse it to just the header. Click + to expand again.

Advanced: Templates

21. How does a message template work?

Each template is made of three parts that are joined together to form the final sent message:

  • Greeting — the opening line, e.g. "Happy Birthday [Tag Person]! 🎉"
  • Sentiment — the personal, heartfelt body of the message.
  • Closing — a brief sign-off, e.g. "Wishing you a wonderful day!"

The Days in Advance setting lets you use a template early (e.g. “1 Day Early”) or as a follow-up (e.g. “1 Day Late”). If you have multiple eligible templates, one is chosen at random to keep messages feeling varied.

Merge tags are placeholders replaced at send time: [Tag Person] (friend’s @mention), [Today] (today’s date), [Image] (random image from your library). See Q9 for the full reference table.

You can attach a specific image to a template using the Attach Image field, or use [Image] anywhere in your text to have a random image from your Image Library inserted at send time. Switch to the Preview tab at any time to see how the assembled message will look before saving.

22. What are timeline posts vs. message templates?

Message templates are sent as comments on Facebook birthday posts or via Messenger. Timeline posts are posts made directly on a friend's Facebook wall/timeline. You can use either or both — they are independent features.

Timeline posts support additional merge tags like {First_Name} and {Last_Name}, font styling options, and image attachments. Create timeline posts from the Templates → Timeline Posts section.
